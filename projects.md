- [Why It's as Hard to Escape an Echo Chamber as It Is to Flee a Cult: Applied Field Guide (C. Thi Nguyen / Aeon)](https://aeon.co/essays/why-its-as-hard-to-escape-an-echo-chamber-as-it-is-to-flee-a-cult)
- [The Epistemology of Fake News: Credibility, Propagation, and Interventions (Sven Bernecker, Amy K. Flowerree, and Thomas Grundmann, eds.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198863977) - Paid book; provides analytical frameworks for diagnosing epistemic pathologies, algorithmic distortion, and conspiracy networks.
